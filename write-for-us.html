Submission Guidelines

We welcome guest contributors who are passionate about technology, AI, innovation, and startups. If you have expertise in these areas and want to share your insights with our readers, we'd love to hear from you.

What We're Looking For

  • Original, well-researched articles on AI, technology, startups, gadgets, or innovation
  • Articles between 1,000-2,500 words
  • Unique perspectives and actionable insights
  • High-quality content with proper citations and references
  • Articles that haven't been published elsewhere

Topics We Cover

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, deep learning, AI applications

🚀 Startups & Innovation

Tech startups, funding, entrepreneurship

📱 Gadgets & Reviews

Product reviews, tech gadgets, devices

🔬 Emerging Technologies

Blockchain, quantum computing, AR/VR

Content Requirements

  • Articles must be original and not published elsewhere
  • Include relevant images (with proper attribution or free-to-use)
  • Use proper heading hierarchy (H2, H3, etc.)
  • Include at least 3-5 external links to authoritative sources
  • Ensure proper grammar and spelling
  • Include a brief author bio (50-100 words)

What to Expect

  • We review all submissions within 5-7 business days
  • We may request edits or revisions before publication
  • You'll receive credit as the author with a bio and social links
  • We reserve the right to edit for clarity, grammar, and SEO
